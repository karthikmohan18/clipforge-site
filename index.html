ClipForge

A personal tool that turns gaming highlights into short vertical clips and publishes them to its owner's own channels.

What it does

  • Picks highlight moments from gaming videos the owner has chosen to clip.
  • Renders them as short 9:16 videos with captions.
  • Uploads the finished clips to the owner's own YouTube channels, and posts them to the owner's own Instagram accounts and Facebook Pages.

Who uses it

ClipForge is not a public service. It is run by its developer for their own channels only, and nobody else can sign in to it.

Google and YouTube access

ClipForge uses YouTube API Services for one thing: uploading videos to the channels its owner has authorized. See the privacy policy for exactly what is accessed and how it is stored.
