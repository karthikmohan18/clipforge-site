Privacy policy

ClipForge · Last updated 13 November 2026

Summary

ClipForge is a personal tool run by its developer for their own channels. It has no public sign-up, no user accounts and no analytics. The only Google data it touches is the permission to upload videos to the YouTube channels its owner has authorized.

What ClipForge accesses

  • YouTube upload permission. ClipForge requests a single Google permission, youtube.upload, which lets it upload videos to an authorized channel. It uses this only to upload clips it has produced.
  • It does not read your watch history, subscriptions, comments, contacts, email or any other Google account data.
  • It also posts the same clips to its owner's own Instagram accounts and Facebook Pages through Meta's APIs, using permissions limited to publishing content.

What is stored, and where

  • The access tokens that Google and Meta issue when a channel is authorized. These are kept as encrypted secrets in the private code repository that runs ClipForge and are used only to publish clips.
  • A log of the clips ClipForge has published (title, link and date), kept in the same private repository.

ClipForge does not collect or store any information about viewers of the clips.

Sharing

ClipForge does not sell, rent or share any data with third parties. Videos are sent to YouTube, Instagram and Facebook solely to publish them on the owner's own channels.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google Account permissions page at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Once access is removed, the stored token stops working.

YouTube API Services

ClipForge uses YouTube API Services. Use of YouTube through ClipForge is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
